Alejandra González Sánchez s a postdoctoral researcher in the EVE group at the [Swiss Tropical and Public Health Institute](https://www.swisstph.ch/en/) in Basel, focusing on the analysis of poliovirus sequences and the development of bioinformatic tools to support that analysis. She also works on non-polio enteroviruses, mainly building Nextclade datasets for these viruses. Alejandra first came to the group as a visiting researcher in 2025, when she worked on these datasets and [Pathoplexus]({{ "/collaborations/pathoplexus/" | relative_url }}), and built up her background in enterovirus genomics, before joining the team as a postdoc.

Before joining EVE, Alejandra worked as a bioinformatician at [Vall d'Hebron University Hospital](https://www.vallhebron.com/) in Barcelona while completing her PhD. Working in a hospital setting gave her a strong public health perspective and let her develop her bioinformatics skills across a wide range of very different viruses. During this time she carried out her doctoral thesis on respiratory syncytial virus (RSV), analysing the evolution of the virus over 13 seasons and evaluating how the introduction of the monoclonal antibody nirsevimab has affected RSV evolution. This work gave her a deep appreciation of viral evolution, of the impact of public health measures, and of the importance of a good surveillance system.

Originally from Spain, Alejandra loves the sun and being outdoors, and she can’t wait to explore the whole of the Alps on foot! When the weather doesn’t cooperate, you’ll find her reading with a good cup of coffee (or hot chocolate).
